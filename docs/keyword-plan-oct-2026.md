## Rules for every post
- At least 1,500 words, one worked example with real numbers, a link to the matching calculator, FAQ, sources named, date and "last updated".
- Use official sources (IRS, SSA, Treasury, CFPB). Do not copy figures from blogs.
- Neutral tone on the political topics (Trump Accounts, overtime): facts and mechanics only.
- After each post: add it to the Related guides map, request indexing (about 10 URLs a day), then check Search Console after one week.

## Honest limits
- The ranking depends on links as well as content, and the site has few. Calculators, clear tables and being early on new topics are the realistic edge.
- Topics with CPC near $0 (COLA, Trump Accounts, overtime) bring visits but little AdSense income. The revenue topics are tax loss harvesting, refinancing, BNPL and mortgages.
