## Weaknesses seen in the competition (our openings)
- calculator.net (debt consolidation): no FAQ, no worked example, no sources, no 2026 data, no comparison table of options,
  no author or date.
- SmartAsset (mortgage): no named author or updated date; the loan-type comparison shows identical 30- and 15-year values
  (placeholder data) and the amortization column headers are misaligned.
- MortgageCalculator.org: "2026" market section cites Q1 2024 data, typos, sources named but not linked, heavy ad/CTA density.
- The Calculator Site (compound): strong E-E-A-T (named creator, credentialed reviewer, references) but inflation option is
  unexplained and it omits the rule of 72.
- NerdWallet (tax): excludes state taxes, hides credits behind "Show Advanced", no worked example.
- Most results have no FAQPage schema; we have it on every tool.

## Where we are behind
Authority and backlinks; number of pages (SmartAsset has 20 state mortgage pages); named author and credentialed reviewer;
embeddable widgets (MortgageCalculator.org distributes widgets and a WordPress plugin for backlinks); brand mentions.

## Strategy by keyword
1. **debt consolidation calculator** (our largest impressions, position about 72): add an options comparison table
   (personal loan, balance transfer, HELOC, debt management plan); guides "does debt consolidation hurt your credit",
   "balance transfer vs personal loan"; a balance-transfer calculator; link from the menu, homepage and related guides.
2. **student loan calculator** (position about 89): a dedicated RAP / income-driven calculator and a guide
   "RAP vs the tiered standard plan (July 2026)"; this is new law, so competition is thin.
3. **mortgage calculator**: a "how much house can I afford" guide; 10 state pages with real, linked property-tax data
   (no placeholder numbers); keep named-author and "last reviewed" visible.
4. **income tax calculator**: a paycheck / take-home calculator by state, scenario comparison (single vs married),
   freelancer and 65+ landing guides; show worked examples above the fold.
5. **auto loan calculator** (position about 25 for the negative-equity query): guide "negative equity trade-in" and a
   cash-back-vs-low-APR calculator.
6. **compound interest**: withdrawals / drawdown mode, rule of 72 and APY vs APR pages, daily vs monthly comparison.

## Cross-cutting
- Named author with real credentials and a reviewer line on every tool (needs the owner's bio and photo).
- "Embed this calculator" widget with an attribution link (earns backlinks the way MortgageCalculator.org does).
- Honest outreach: Quora/Reddit answers, LinkedIn posts, data stories (for example 2026 tax bracket explainers).
- Re-run this crawl and the Search Console position report every 7 days; compare words, schema and positions.
